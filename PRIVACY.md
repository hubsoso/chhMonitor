# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**扩展名称 / Extension:** CHH 二手区上新提醒（非官方）
**最后更新 / Last updated:** 2026-07-15

---

## 中文

本扩展是一个**非官方的第三方工具**，与 Chiphell 论坛无任何关联。

### 我们收集哪些数据
**不收集任何个人数据。** 本扩展不采集、不存储、不上传你的任何个人信息、浏览历史或身份数据。

### 数据如何处理
- 扩展仅向你所关注的论坛版块页面（`https://www.chiphell.com`）发起**只读**请求，用于检测是否有新帖。
- 该请求使用**你本机浏览器中已有的登录状态（Cookie）**，与你自己手动访问该网站的方式一致；扩展不会读取、保存或传输你的账号密码或 Cookie。
- 你的**设置项**（刷新周期、关键词、发帖人、是否弹窗）和**已读记录**（帖子 ID）仅保存在**你本机的浏览器本地存储**（`chrome.storage.local`）中，**不会上传到任何服务器**。

### 数据共享
本扩展**不与任何第三方共享数据**，不含任何分析、追踪、广告 SDK，也不使用任何远程代码。

### 权限说明
- `storage`：本地保存你的设置与已读记录。
- `alarms`：按你设定的周期定时检查新帖。
- `notifications`：在你开启时弹出新帖桌面通知。
- `host_permissions (chiphell.com)`：读取被监控版块页面以检测新帖。

### 联系方式
如有疑问，请通过 Chrome Web Store 商店页的开发者邮箱联系。

## English

This extension is an **unofficial third-party tool** and is **not affiliated with** the Chiphell forum.

### Data we collect
**None.** This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or identity data.

### How data is handled
- The extension only makes **read-only** requests to the forum page (`https://www.chiphell.com`) to detect new posts.
- These requests use **the login session (cookies) already present in your own browser**, exactly as if you visited the site yourself. The extension never reads, stores, or transmits your credentials or cookies.
- Your **settings** (refresh interval, keywords, authors, notification toggle) and **read state** (post IDs) are stored **only in your browser's local storage** (`chrome.storage.local`) and are **never uploaded to any server**.

### Data sharing
This extension **does not share any data with third parties**. It contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, and executes no remote code.

### Permissions
- `storage`: store your settings and read state locally.
- `alarms`: periodically check for new posts on your chosen interval.
- `notifications`: show desktop notifications for new posts when enabled.
- `host_permissions (chiphell.com)`: read the monitored forum page to detect new posts.

### Contact
For questions, contact the developer via the email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
